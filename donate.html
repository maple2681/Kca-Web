Make a Donation to KCA

Join us today in securing KCA’s future of partnering with missionary families through Christian education and a supportive community. We believe the Lord is preserving the school through these difficult days.

The work and ministry to serve Ukrainians in rebuilding and healing from this war will be expansive. KCA will be there in the critical role alongside those families who are answering God’s call. Thank you for your donation to the work of KCA.

KCA partners with Bright Fund Ukraine, a 501(c)(3) tax exempt organization, EIN 86-1627924, that accepts donations on behalf of KCA and its ministries in Ukraine.
