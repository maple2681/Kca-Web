Requirements

What we look for

Faith

“A biblically integrated curriculum is most effective when delivered by educators with a strong biblical worldview.” We seek teachers with a personal faith in Jesus Christ.

Education

A Bachelor’s degree is required. A degree in Education and ACSI Teacher Certification are preferred but not mandatory.

Support

  • Assistance with living expenses, as the school budget allows
  • A collaborative, Christ-centered environment
  • Professional and spiritual support from staff

Visas & arrival

KCA can invite foreigners to Ukraine as volunteers but cannot provide work visas. Many teachers serve through mission agencies such as RCE International. New staff are expected to arrive in August.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a teaching certificate?

A Bachelor’s degree is required. A degree in Education and ACSI certification are preferred but not mandatory.

Can KCA provide a work visa?

No. KCA can invite foreigners as volunteers but cannot provide work visas. Many teachers come through mission agencies such as RCE International.

Is the position paid?

KCA offers assistance with living expenses, depending on the school budget. Please contact us for details.

When would I start?

New teachers are expected to arrive in August before the school year begins.

Who can I talk to?

Email recruiter@kca.org.ua or office@kca.org.ua, or call +38 (097) 359 7467.

Is God calling you to Kyiv?

We’d love to talk with you about serving at KCA.
