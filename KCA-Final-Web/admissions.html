Applications are open for the 2026–2027 school year

KCA welcomes students from Pre-School (age 3) through Grade 12. Enrollment opens March 1.

Send an inquiry Tuition
Who Can Enroll

Admission priority

Places are offered in the following order of priority:

Current & sponsoring-mission families

Current students and applicants from KCA’s Sponsoring Missions.

KCA staff

Children of KCA staff members.

Missionary families

Missionary families relocating to Kyiv.

Expatriate & local families

Expatriate families in Kyiv, then local English-as-a-foreign-language speakers (subject to placement testing).

What we ask of families

  • Agree with KCA’s Christian philosophy of education
  • Fulfil financial obligations to the school
  • Take part in a family visit and placement testing

Please note

KCA is not able to accommodate students with severe learning disabilities, emotional disturbances, or behavioral challenges that conflict with the school’s expectations. KCA reserves the right to decline admission if necessary.

How to Apply

How to apply

  1. Contact us

    Reach the admissions office by phone or email, or send the inquiry form below.

  2. Apply online

    Submit the online application together with the required student forms and records.

  3. Visit & placement

    Schedule a family visit, campus tour and placement testing.

  4. Sign the agreement

    Sign the annual enrollment agreement.

  5. Initial payment

    Submit the initial payment to confirm your child’s place.

FAQ

Common questions

When can we apply?

KCA is accepting applications for the 2026–2027 school year, beginning March 1.

What ages do you accept?

Students from Pre-School (age 3) through Grade 12.

Does my child need to speak English?

All classes are taught in English. Local English-as-a-foreign-language speakers are admitted subject to placement testing.

Is there an entrance test?

Yes. Placement testing is part of the family visit, so we can place your child in the right grade and support them well.

Do we need to be Christians to apply?

Parents are asked to agree with KCA’s Christian philosophy of education, as every subject is taught from a Biblical worldview.

Who do I contact?

Admissions: +38 (063) 723 67 38 · admissions@kca.org.ua

Would you like to see the campus first?

We’d love to show you around and answer your questions in person.
