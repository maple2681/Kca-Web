Elementary · K–5

Elementary school

What elementary students learn

  • Math: SRA Real Math (McGraw Hill)
  • Language Arts K–2: Sing, Spell, Read & Write
  • Language Arts 3–5: literature-based, with Easy Grammar, Spelling Workout and IEW writing
  • Science: God’s World (K) and Science Fusion (1–5)
  • Social Studies: unit studies with Abeka (K) and Veritas Press (1–5)
  • Bible: Bible curriculum (K–1) and Veritas Bible Series (2–5)
  • Ukrainian language: teacher-developed materials

Every week elementary students also have computer, art, music and physical education classes.
