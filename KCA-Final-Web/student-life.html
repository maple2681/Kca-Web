After-School Program

After-school care for K–5

After-School Program

Our after-school program runs daily from 3:15 to 6:30 p.m. for children in kindergarten through fifth grade.

  • Outdoor recreation and LEGO activities
  • Educational activities and homework time
  • Shared mealtimes together
  • Optional art, music and sports clubs (extra cost)
  • Annual enrollment or hourly rates for occasional use
