Support KCA

Your gift helps KCA keep partnering with missionary families through Christian education, and supports our ministry to Ukrainians who are rebuilding and healing from this war.

Where your gift goes

Missionary families

Help missionary parents keep serving in Ukraine while their children receive a quality education.

Christian education

Support English-language teaching within a Biblical worldview for students from Pre-K to Grade 12.

Healing & rebuilding

Stand with Ukrainian families as they rebuild and heal from the war.

Tax-deductible giving from the U.S.

KCA partners with Bright Fund Ukraine, a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization (EIN 86-1627924) that accepts donations on behalf of KCA.

  • Secure online gift through the official donation form
  • Check payable to Bright Fund Ukraine
  • Wire transfer (the Finance Office will send details)
